\item \textbf{APG Insight} {\color{muted}(architect; Python, dbt, PostgreSQL, DuckDB)}. A monthly pipeline that works out what people ask a nonprofit's AI chat about, with no fixed category list: multilingual embeddings are clustered into 40 micro-topics and merged into 12 categories, a local open-weight model (Qwen3-4B) judges whether each is a real subject and names it, and every message joins up to three. Categories are stability-checked across refits and keep their keys from run to run. \textbf{Discovery and assignment for 120,901 messages take about two minutes on a laptop; a 27 GB, 5.14-million-row dump is read in 2.5 minutes without being restored.}
\item \textbf{Subtopics, tone and conversations.} Each run splits every large, stable category into named subtopics up to three levels deep. An optional tone stage scores messages from Hostile to Friendly: a local model scores a sample (the teacher), a classifier trained on those scores labels the rest (the student), the student is checked out of fold, and a category it cannot judge is withheld. dbt traces conversations from message structure, so topic and tone roll up per conversation.
\item \textbf{A public portal that answers questions without exposing anyone.} A visitor asks what a month talked about; the question is matched against the month's message vectors and the matches are split into topics that come from the data (no taxonomy in the code, and no language model sees the question). A count appears only when ten different people stand behind it, and each secret has one reader, so a compromised portal (Next.js over a read-only API) cannot reach the pipeline or the text. Built and tested end to end; not yet deployed.
\end{itemize}
