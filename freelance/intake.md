# Written intake

Thanks for considering me. This intake replaces a discovery call. Answer on your own time, in as much or as little detail as you like; short answers are fine. A call is always available if you would rather talk, but it is never required.

When I have your answers, I reply with a written scope: what I will deliver, a fixed price, a date, and an acceptance checklist, so we both know what "done" means before any work starts.

## 1. The outcome

1. What do you need to be true when this engagement ends? One or two sentences is enough.
2. Why now? What happens if it waits three months?
3. How will you judge whether it worked? A number, a passed review, a team using it, or something else.

## 2. What exists today

4. What is already built or in place? Links to a repository, a diagram or a document all help.
5. Which systems does this touch? For example your identity provider (Okta, Entra ID), cloud (Azure, AWS, GCP), AI vendors, and data stores.
6. What has already been tried, and what did you learn from it?

## 3. Constraints

7. Where must data stay? For example "no data leaves our network" or "only approved vendors".
8. Which compliance frameworks apply, if any? For example SOC 2, HIPAA, SOX or GDPR.
9. Is there a hard date, and what is it tied to?
10. Is there a budget range? It helps me propose the right size of scope rather than the largest one.

## 4. People

11. Who will own this after I hand it over, and how much time can they give to the handoff?
12. Who approves the scope and signs off the acceptance checklist?
13. How does your team prefer to communicate: the platform's messages, email, a shared channel, or a mix?

## 5. Anything else

14. Is there anything you would want me to know that the questions above missed?

---

**What happens next.** I read everything, ask any follow-up questions in writing, then send the scope. If the work is not a good fit for me, I will say so plainly and, where I can, suggest someone better suited.

**About AI use.** I build with AI coding agents under the same tests and review gates I would hold a person to, and I review and sign off on every deliverable myself. If your work is sensitive, tell me here and I will switch off any platform or vendor setting that would let your code or messages train a model.
