# Message templates

Recurring messages, written once so the tone is settled in advance. Each one leads with the fact, gives the reason, and ends with a clear next step. Fill in the braces and send.

## Status update

Send on the agreed day, even when there is little news.

```text
Hi {name}, a quick status for {engagement}.

Done since last update: {items}.
Next: {items}, on track for {date}.
Risks: {none / one line each, with what I'm doing about it}.
Anything I need from you: {none / the specific item and by when}.
```

## Risk note

Send the day a risk is found, before it becomes a missed date.

```text
Hi {name}, I want to flag a risk early so you have time to decide.

What I found: {the fact, in one or two sentences}.
Why it matters: {the effect on scope, date or quality}.
Options:
  A. {option}, which means {trade-off}.
  B. {option}, which means {trade-off}.
My recommendation is {A/B}, because {reason}. I'll hold on this part until you choose; everything else continues.
```

## Scope change

When a new request arrives mid-engagement.

```text
Hi {name}, thanks for this. It's a good addition, and it's outside the scope we agreed, so I want to handle it cleanly rather than let it slip in.

Two ways to do it:
  1. Add it to this engagement: {extra price}, and the finish date moves to {date}.
  2. Finish the current scope as agreed, then take this on as a separate, fixed-scope follow-up.

Either works for me. Which would you prefer?
```

## Out of scope

When a request is outside what I do, or outside what I would do well.

```text
Hi {name}, thanks for asking. That one isn't a good fit for me: {one honest reason, such as "it needs a licensed auditor" or "it's front-end design, which isn't my specialty"}.

What I'd suggest instead: {a type of specialist, or a specific referral}. I'm glad to write a short brief for them so nothing gets lost.
```

## Handoff note

Send with the final delivery.

```text
Hi {name}, {engagement} is complete. Here is everything in one place.

Delivered: {list, with links}.
Acceptance checklist: {each item, marked done, with where to see it}.
How to run it: {link to the runbook}.
Known limits: {anything a new owner should know on day one}.
If something breaks: {what to check first}.

It's been a pleasure working on this. If a question comes up after the engagement, write to me and I'll answer it.
```

## Declining a vague brief

```text
Hi {name}, thanks for thinking of me. As written, I can't scope this well enough to give you a fixed price I'd stand behind, and I'd rather tell you that now than guess.

If you can share {the one or two missing facts}, I'll gladly take another look.
```
